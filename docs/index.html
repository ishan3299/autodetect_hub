About Platform

AutoDetect Hub acts as a centralized Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) engine. It automatically aggregates indicators from open-source feeds (URLhaus, MalwareBazaar, Feodo Tracker), normalizes the data, and generates detection rules for SIEM/IDS platforms.
